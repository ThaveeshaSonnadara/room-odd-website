# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are high-net-worth homeowners, commercial developers, hoteliers, and design-focused individuals looking for chartered architectural consultancy services in Sri Lanka and internationally. They seek bespoke, timeless architectural design, spatial planning, interior architecture, and project oversight.

## Product Purpose

Room ODD's website presents an architectural consultancy studio with authority, elegance, and clarity. It communicates room-odd's design philosophy, portfolio of curated projects, service offerings, multi-step design process, leadership team, journal insights, and client testimonials, enabling visitors to easily book a consultation.

## Positioning

Chartered architectural consultancy based in Colombo, Sri Lanka. Grounded in "Architecture as an Act of Optimism" — designing for permanence, context, natural materials, and tropical modernism with unyielding craftsmanship.

## Operating Context

Web desktop and high-end mobile experience. Visited by clients evaluating luxury architectural services, reviewing past project case studies, and scheduling architectural consultations.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js 15 App Router (React 19) + Tailwind CSS + Framer Motion
- Clean, minimal 8pt grid architectural design system with blueprint rules
- Direct contact form submission backed by Resend API integration (`new-consultation-request` template)
- Dynamic route routing for 13 full pages (`/`, `/about`, `/services`, `/projects`, `/projects/[slug]`, `/process`, `/team`, `/testimonials`, `/blog`, `/blog/[slug]`, `/contact`, `/privacy-policy`, `/terms`)

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Room ODD (Pvt) Ltd
- Tagline: Chartered Architectural Consultancy · Sri Lanka
- Colors: Warm White Canvas (`#FAFAFA`), Deep Charcoal (`#121316`), Muted Bronze (`#B38051`), Natural Olive (`#2A382E`), Concrete Beige (`#F0ECE1`)
- Typography: Cormorant Garamond serif headings, Inter body
- Visual aesthetic: Architectural minimalism, blueprint grid rules, high contrast, non-glossy, non-digital.

## Evidence on Hand

- Verified project case studies: Villa Serenity, The Monolith Studio, Bamboo Pavilion, Urban Sanctuary, Cinnamon Heights, Horizon Gallery
- Real team profiles, client testimonials, and process phase breakdowns
- Live Resend API integration for consultation booking requests

## Product Principles

1. **Architecture is an Act of Optimism**: Every space elevates human experience.
2. **Contextual & Climate Sensitivity**: Tropical modernism designed for site, light, and natural materials.
3. **Impeccable Craft & Permanence**: Meticulous execution, zero digital clutter or artificial AI tells.
